Eagra Labs / Behind FullPlan

Built from the reality of project delivery.

Eagra Labs is the Irish product studio behind FullPlan. We bring delivery experience, systems thinking and software design to the challenge of planning complex work.

01 / Why FullPlan

A plan should connect the team.

Complex projects span systems, functions and hand-offs. When schedules, resource assumptions and constraints sit in separate places, it becomes harder to see what delivery depends on.

FullPlan brings those conversations into a shared visual workspace, from individual activities to the wider site schedule.

What guides FullPlan
Make the work visible.Keep ownership clear.Connect the hand-offs.Learn from real delivery.
02 / How we build

Close to the people doing the work.

We develop FullPlan through practical use, user testing and feedback from the people planning and delivering projects.

Eagra Labs is the product and trading brand of Goodson Commissioning Technologies, an Irish-registered company based in Ireland and serving customers internationally.
